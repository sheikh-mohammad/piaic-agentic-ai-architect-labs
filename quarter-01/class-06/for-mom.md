# For Mom

Your hands, the first warmth I knew,
A steady guide in all I do.
Through every fall and every climb,
Your love has been my constant time.

You taught me strength in gentle grace,
And courage found in a soft embrace.
When storms would rise and skies turned grey,
You were the light that showed the way.

The quiet prayers, the sleepless nights,
The sacrifices out of sight.
Each wrinkle tells a story true —
A map of all the love in you.

So here's my heart, in words so small,
For giving me your all-in-all.
No poem long enough could be,
To hold the love you've given me.
